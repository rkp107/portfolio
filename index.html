01 — About

A bit about me

I'm an ASIC verification engineer who enjoys the puzzle of proving a design is correct before a single wafer is etched. My work spans block- and SoC-level verification of video codec IPs, high-speed protocols, and security and clock/reset subsystems.

With an M.Tech in Microelectronics & VLSI Design from IIT Gandhinagar, I've built UVM testbenches from scratch, driven constrained-random and formal coverage closure, and uncovered critical RTL and C-model bugs. Lately I've been exploring LLM-based stimulus generation to make coverage closure faster, and I enjoy mentoring new engineers and improving team automation along the way.
